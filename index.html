About Us

The Team Behind the Magic

We're a tight-knit team of designers, developers, and strategists passionate about creating digital experiences that matter.

Our Story

Founded in 2017, Kwen Design started as a two-person passion project in a small studio. Today, we're a 12-member team that has delivered over 250 projects for clients across 20+ industries. Our mission is simple: bridge the gap between visionary ideas and exceptional digital execution.

We believe great design isn't just about aesthetics — it's about solving problems, creating connections, and driving measurable business outcomes. Every pixel, every line of code, and every strategic decision we make is guided by this philosophy.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers. If you don't find what you're looking for, feel free to contact us.

Project timelines vary based on scope and complexity. A landing page typically takes 1-2 weeks, a multi-page website 4-6 weeks, and a full-scale web application 2-4 months. We provide a detailed timeline during the discovery phase and stick to it.

We work with a wide range of modern technologies including React, Next.js, Vue.js, Node.js, Python, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, and cloud platforms like AWS and Vercel. We choose the best tech stack for each project's specific needs.

We work with businesses of all sizes — from early-stage startups to Fortune 500 enterprises. Our flexible pricing and scalable processes allow us to adapt to any project scope and budget.

Post-launch support includes bug fixes, performance monitoring, security updates, content updates, and analytics reporting. We offer monthly retainer plans for ongoing support or project-based maintenance packages.

Absolutely. We regularly work within existing design systems and brand guidelines. We can also help extend or improve your current system to ensure consistency across all touchpoints.

We use a combination of Slack for daily communication, weekly video calls for progress reviews, and project management tools like Notion or Linear for task tracking. You'll always know exactly where your project stands.
