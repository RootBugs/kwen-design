About Kwen Design

We're a team of passionate designers and developers building the digital future.

Founded in 2017 in Mumbai, Kwen Design started as a two-person passion project. Today, we're a 12-member team of designers, developers, and strategists who've delivered 250+ projects for clients ranging from ambitious startups to Fortune 500 enterprises.

We believe great design isn't just about aesthetics — it's about solving real problems, creating emotional connections, and driving measurable business outcomes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers. If you don't find what you're looking for, feel free to contact us.

Project timelines vary based on scope. A landing page takes 1-2 weeks, a multi-page website 4-6 weeks, and a full-scale web application 2-4 months. We provide a detailed timeline during discovery and stick to it.

We work with React, Next.js, Vue.js, Node.js, Python, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, and cloud platforms like AWS and Vercel. We choose the best tech stack for each project's specific needs.

We work with businesses of all sizes — from early-stage startups to Fortune 500 enterprises. Our flexible pricing and scalable processes allow us to adapt to any project scope and budget.

Post-launch support includes bug fixes, performance monitoring, security updates, content updates, and analytics reporting. We offer monthly retainer plans for ongoing support or project-based maintenance packages.
